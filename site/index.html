The Stopped Clock

Første testsak i formatet tidslinje på plantegning. Spillet er på engelsk, testnotatene på norsk. En solver har bekreftet at saken har nøyaktig én løsning med disse fire ledetrådene.

Mr. Harlow is dead

  • Each hour, every room holds exactly one suspect.
  • Between hours, a suspect either stays put or walks through one door into the next room.
  • Mr. Harlow died in the Library when the clock stopped. Whoever was there at that hour is the killer.

Evidence

  1. 1The Library clock was knocked over. It stopped at 23:00.
  2. 2Bram's pruning shears lie by the body. He found Mr. Harlow at midnight.
  3. 3The dishwater is still warm. Dex was washing up in the Kitchen at midnight.
  4. 4Ada's floury footprints never reach the Library or the Conservatory.
  5. 5A thread from Cora's shawl and a button from Dex's waistcoat hang on the same doorframe. At 22:30 they squeezed past each other and swapped rooms.
